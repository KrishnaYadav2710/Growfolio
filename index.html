HOW IT WORKS

Methodology

CSV input

Required columns: Symbol, Quantity, AvgPrice. Optional research columns: Sector, PE, ROE, ROCE, DebtEquity, RevenueGrowth, ProfitGrowth, Volatility, CurrentPrice.

Market prices

Growfolio first uses CurrentPrice from the CSV. “Refresh prices” then attempts a public Yahoo Finance quote request for NSE symbols (.NS). Browser/CORS availability can change, so a static GitHub Pages site cannot guarantee live quotes. For production, use a licensed market-data API through a small serverless proxy.

Rating model

The 0–100 research score combines profitability, growth, leverage, valuation and price/risk characteristics. A–E ratings summarize the score. The model is intentionally visible in app.js and does not claim to predict returns.

Risk buckets

Holdings are grouped into High risk · High return, Quality growth, Stable / defensive, or Needs review using volatility, growth, leverage and quality metrics supplied in the CSV.
